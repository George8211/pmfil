Why Sintered Metal

Filtration Media Built from Metal Powder, Not Fibers

Powder sintering bonds metal particles into a rigid porous body. The result is a filter medium with characteristics fundamentally different from wire mesh or fiber-based media.

Porous Metal Structure

A rigid, interconnected pore network formed by sintering metal powder — stable geometry that holds its shape under differential pressure.

Mechanical Strength

The sintered matrix is load-bearing and resistant to vibration, flow surges, and handling compared with flexible media.

Temperature & Chemical Resistance

All-metal construction suits applications where polymer-based media cannot survive — subject to material and process conditions.

Cleanable & Reusable

Sintered metal elements can typically be cleaned and regenerated in place, supporting long service life when properly maintained.
