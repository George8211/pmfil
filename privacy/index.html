Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

When you submit the Request Information form or email us, we receive the details you provide: typically your name, email address, company, country, product interest, and any technical or project details you choose to share.

How We Use It

  • To respond to your inquiry and communicate about your request.
  • To prepare a quotation or technical proposal where applicable.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes. Inquiry data is handled only by the people involved in answering it.

Form Processing

This is a static website without its own server-side database. Form submissions are processed by a third-party form-delivery service solely to route your message to our inbox, and are subject to that service's own data processing terms.

Data Retention

Inquiry correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting business relationship, then deleted.

Your Rights

You may request a copy, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing sales@pmfil.com.

Cookies & Analytics

This website sets no tracking cookies and runs no advertising trackers. If analytics are added in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy: sales@pmfil.com.
