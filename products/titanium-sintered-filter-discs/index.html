Titanium Sintered Filter Discs

Titanium sintered filter discs — corrosion-resistant porous metal discs for aggressive fluid filtration and high-purity duties. Custom sizes available upon request.

Request Information Form Overview

Product Introduction

Titanium sintered filter discs are flat porous metal components sintered from titanium powder. They combine the rigid, regenerable, depth-filtration behavior of sintered metal discs with titanium’s outstanding corrosion resistance, making them the material of choice when the fluid would pit, crevice-corrode, or contaminate a stainless steel disc.

Construction

  1. Metal powder — titanium powder of controlled particle size sets the pore structure of the finished disc.
  2. Forming — the powder is formed into flat compacts at the required diameter and thickness.
  3. Sintering — carried out under vacuum or inert atmosphere; atmosphere control is essential for titanium to achieve clean bonds between particles.
  4. Porous structure — open interconnected pores extend through the disc thickness for depth filtration.
  5. Support / sealing interface — edge finishing for clamping, gasketing, or welding into the housing per the application.

Key Characteristics

Corrosion Resistance

Titanium withstands chloride-bearing and many other aggressive media that attack stainless steels.

Depth Filtration

Retention occurs through the full disc thickness, giving high dirt capacity in a flat format.

Low Density

Lighter than steel equivalents — an advantage in compact instruments and equipment.

Mechanical Strength

High specific strength resists differential pressure and clamping loads.

Purity

Adds no heavy-metal contamination, supporting high-purity and pharmaceutical duties.

Cleanability

Regenerable by backflushing, ultrasonic, or chemical cleaning for repeated reuse.
