Sintered Titanium Filter Elements

Sintered titanium filter elements made from powder — porous metal filter elements valued for high chemical resistance and low density. Custom dimensions available upon request.

Request Information Form Overview

Product Introduction

Sintered titanium filter elements are porous metal filter components produced by sintering titanium powder into a rigid monolithic body. Titanium’s combination of high chemical resistance, low density, and excellent strength-to-weight ratio makes sintered titanium elements a natural choice for processes where stainless steels reach their corrosion limits — most notably in chloride-bearing and other aggressive environments.

Construction

The construction follows the same powder metallurgy route as other powder sintered elements, with titanium-specific process control:

  1. Metal powder — titanium powder with a controlled particle size distribution is selected to achieve the target pore structure.
  2. Forming — the powder is compacted or filled into molds in the required element shape.
  3. Sintering — the compact is heated in a high-vacuum or inert-atmosphere furnace. Titanium is highly reactive at sintering temperature, so atmosphere control is critical to achieving clean particle bonds.
  4. Porous structure — the sintered body retains an open, interconnected network of pores derived from the voids between powder particles.
  5. Connection / support structure — end fittings or mounting interfaces are applied according to the installation requirements.

The passive oxide film that gives titanium its corrosion resistance forms naturally on the sintered surfaces as well, so the pore walls benefit from the same chemical protection as bulk titanium.

Key Characteristics

Porous Metal Structure

A rigid pore network formed by sintering titanium powder into a self-supporting monolithic element.

High Chemical Resistance

Titanium's protective oxide surface gives outstanding resistance to many aggressive media, especially chloride-containing environments.

Low Density

Titanium is roughly half the density of steel, an advantage in weight-sensitive installations.

Mechanical Strength

The sintered titanium matrix provides high specific strength and handles differential pressure and vibration.

Cleanability

Pores can typically be regenerated by backwashing, ultrasonic, or chemical cleaning methods.

Reusability

Regenerable elements offer long service life in suitable applications when properly maintained.
