Titanium Sintered Spargers

Titanium sintered spargers — corrosion-resistant porous metal aeration elements for aggressive gas-liquid dispersion duties. Custom designs available upon request.

Request Information Form Overview

Product Introduction

Titanium sintered spargers bring the fine-bubble dispersion of porous metal sparging to corrosive duties where stainless steel reaches its limits. The sintered titanium body provides the pore network that turns an incoming gas stream into a uniform bubble cloud, while titanium’s natural oxide film protects the pore surfaces in chloride-bearing and other aggressive liquids.

Construction

  1. Metal powder — titanium powder with a controlled particle size distribution determines the pore structure of the finished sparger.
  2. Forming & sintering — the powder is shaped (tube, disc, ring, or custom form) and sintered under vacuum or inert atmosphere, a critical step for reactive titanium.
  3. Porous structure — open, interconnected pores distribute gas across the whole wetted surface and generate fine bubbles.
  4. Gas inlet / support structure — fittings match the vessel or piping installation.

As with all porous spargers, the actual bubble size in service depends on pore structure, gas flow, liquid properties, and pressure — share these with us when inquiring so the appropriate pore grade can be discussed.

Key Characteristics

Corrosion Resistance

Titanium's passive oxide layer resists chloride-bearing and many other aggressive process liquids where stainless steels may pit or crevice-corrode.

Fine Bubble Generation

The sintered pore network disperses gas into a fine, uniform bubble cloud for efficient gas-liquid contact.

Low Density

Low specific weight eases handling and reduces the load on vessel mountings.

Mechanical Strength

High strength-to-weight ratio handles pressure load and vibration robustly.

Cleanability

Pores can typically be regenerated by backflushing or chemical cleaning.

Purity

Titanium adds no heavy-metal ions to the process fluid, supporting contamination-sensitive duties.
