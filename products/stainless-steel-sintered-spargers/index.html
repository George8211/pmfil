Stainless Steel Sintered Spargers

Stainless steel sintered spargers — porous metal aeration elements for fine, uniform gas dispersion into liquids. Custom designs available upon request.

Request Information Form Overview

Product Introduction

Stainless steel sintered spargers are porous metal aeration elements that disperse gas into liquids as a fine, uniform bubble cloud. They are manufactured by sintering stainless steel powder into a rigid porous body with a controlled pore network, then fitting it with a gas inlet connection. When pressurized gas is fed to the inlet, the porous wall distributes the flow across its entire surface and releases it through countless micron-scale pores.

Construction

  1. Metal powder — stainless steel powder of controlled particle size is chosen to set the pore structure, which in turn influences bubble formation.
  2. Forming & sintering — the powder is formed into the sparger shape (tube, disc, ring, or custom geometry) and sintered below melting point so particles bond into a rigid porous matrix.
  3. Porous structure — interconnected open pores provide the huge number of parallel gas release points that create fine, even bubbling.
  4. Gas inlet / support structure — an integral or welded fitting connects the sparger to the gas supply and anchors it in the vessel.

The bubble size produced in service depends not only on pore structure but also on gas flow rate, liquid properties, and operating pressure — these should be reviewed together when selecting a sparger.

Key Characteristics

Fine Bubble Generation

The sintered pore network breaks incoming gas into a cloud of small bubbles, maximizing gas-liquid contact area.

Uniform Dispersion

Thousands of pores distributed across the element surface release gas evenly, avoiding channeling and dead zones.

Mechanical Strength

The rigid sintered body withstands installation loads, vibration, and thermal cycling in vessels and piping.

Temperature & Chemical Resistance

All-stainless construction suits elevated-temperature and chemically demanding processes within material limits.

Cleanability

Pores can typically be cleared of fouling by backflushing, chemical cleaning, or ultrasonic treatment.

Design Flexibility

Tubes, discs, rings, and custom shapes can be sintered with integrated fittings for the vessel design.

Applications

  • Aeration and oxygenation of process liquids
  • Gas dispersion in chemical reactors
  • Sparging in pharmaceutical and bioprocess vessels
  • Carbonation and gas injection in food and beverage processing
  • Stripping and gas-liquid contacting operations

Suitability for a specific application always depends on actual process conditions.
