Sintered Metal Filter Products

PMFIL covers one product category only: metal powder sintered filter elements. Every product is a porous metal component formed by sintering stainless steel or titanium powder, and each is available in three product forms — filter elements, spargers, and filter discs/plates. This focus is deliberate: we would rather cover one filtration technology in depth than catalogue many unrelated products.

Select a product form below, then choose the material — sintered stainless steel or sintered titanium — that matches your process conditions.
