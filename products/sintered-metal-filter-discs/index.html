Product Introduction

Sintered metal filter discs and plates are flat porous metal components produced by sintering metal powder into sheet or disc form. They provide the same rigid, cleanable, all-metal filtration medium as tubular elements, but in a flat geometry that suits inline discs, instrument protection, analytical equipment, and compact filter housings.

Depending on thickness and diameter, these components may be referred to as discs, plates, or sheets — the naming follows industry usage, and we use the term “discs” on this site as the generic product name. In practice, suitability for a given duty is defined by pore structure, thickness, and dimensions rather than by the name.

Construction

A sintered metal filter disc is made by forming metal powder into a flat compact and sintering it so the particles bond into a rigid porous plate:

  • Porous disc body — the sintered powder matrix provides filtration through its entire thickness (depth filtration) rather than only at a surface.
  • Pore structure — controlled by powder particle size and sintering conditions, graded to the required retention level.
  • Support / sealing interface — discs are typically clamped, gasketed, or welded into housings; rim designs can be adapted to the sealing concept.

Thicker plates and multi-layer sintered combinations are used where higher mechanical load or graded filtration is required.
