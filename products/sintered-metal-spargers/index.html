Sintered Metal Spargers

Sintered metal spargers — porous stainless steel and titanium aeration elements for gas dispersion and gas-liquid contact in industrial processes.

Product Introduction

A sintered metal sparger is a porous metal component that introduces gas into a liquid as a dispersion of small bubbles. Instead of releasing gas through a single open pipe — which produces large, uneven bubbles — the sparger forces the gas through millions of micron-scale pores in a sintered metal body. The pore network breaks the gas stream into a fine, uniform bubble cloud, dramatically increasing the gas-liquid contact area.

Construction

A sintered metal sparger combines the same powder sintering technology used for filter elements with a gas-distribution function:

  • Porous sintered body — stainless steel or titanium powder sintered into a tube, disc, ring, or custom shape, with a controlled open-pore structure.
  • Gas inlet connection — a threaded or flanged fitting that connects the sparger to the gas supply line; the gas plenum feeds the porous wall evenly.
  • Mounting interface — flanges, threads, or welded fittings that secure the sparger in the vessel or piping.

The porous wall acts as a flow restrictor: every pore releases gas at a similar local condition, which is what produces uniform bubble size distribution across the sparger surface.
