About PMFIL

PMFIL (pmfil.com) is an independent specialist website focused on one product category: metal powder sintered filter elements — porous metal filtration components made by sintering stainless steel or titanium powder.

We deliberately keep a narrow scope: three product forms (filter elements, spargers, and filter discs/plates) built on two material platforms (sintered stainless steel powder and sintered titanium powder). Everything published here — product pages and the Technical Resources knowledge center — exists to help engineers and buyers understand, select, and specify this filtration technology correctly.

What This Site Is — and Is Not

PMFIL is not a general filtration catalog, and it is not an online store. It is a technical resource and inquiry channel: readers arrive through search, learn from in-depth technical articles, review product pages, and contact us with their requirements.

We follow a simple content policy: original writing, publicly established industry knowledge, and no invented specifications. Where a parameter depends on design or application, we say so rather than quote a number. Product photographs on this site are schematic illustrations until verified product photography becomes available.

Contact

For product inquiries, technical questions, or custom configuration requests, use the Request Information form or email sales@pmfil.com. We typically reply within 1–2 business days.
